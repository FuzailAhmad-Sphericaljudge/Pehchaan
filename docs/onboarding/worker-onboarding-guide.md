## 1. Welcome and what Pehchaan is

🖼️ *[ILLUSTRATION PLACEHOLDER — a worker holding a phone, NGO helper beside them]*

Say in your own words:

- "Pehchaan ek mobile app hai jo aapki kaam ki jaankari aapke haath mein rakhti hai."
- Your wage records, your daily safety check-in, and your complaints — all in
  one place, in **your language** (show the language picker: हिंदी / English /
  বাংলা / தமிழ் / తెలుగు).
- Pehchaan is run with NGO caseworkers — real people review every complaint.
- **Important (say this clearly):** Pehchaan does not replace emergency
  services, police, courts, or labour departments. **In immediate danger, call 112.**

## 2. Sign up — phone number and code

🖼️ *[SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER — Register screen with phone field]*

| On screen | What to tell workers |
|---|---|
| Phone number field | Enter your own mobile number |
| **Get code** button | You will get an SMS with a 6-digit code |
| Code boxes | Type the code from the SMS |
| Language picker | Choose your language — the whole app follows it |

- Demo/failed SMS? The coordinator's demo phone can show the code (explain
  honestly that this is only for the session).
- No email, no password to remember. **Your phone number is your account.**
- Privacy line to say out loud: "Aapka number NGO caseworkers tak hi simit
  hai — koi employer ya company ko nahi diya jata."

## 3. Log a wage entry

🖼️ *[SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER — Add wage screen]*

Steps as built in the app:

1. Open **Wages → Add entry**.
2. Fill: date, amount paid, work category, employer/site (pick from your
   profile; you can add one).
3. Save.

What workers should know:

- Every entry builds **your own wage record** — your proof of what you were
  paid and when.
- If the amount looks below the state's minimum wage for your category, the
  app shows a **friendly message** and asks if you want to file a complaint.
  Nothing is filed automatically — **you decide**.
- Before your **first wage entry, check-in, or complaint** the app shows a
  short consent notice — read it together, then tap agree. 🖼️ *[SCREENSHOT — consent screen]*

## 4. Daily safety check-in

🖼️ *[ILLUSTRATION PLACEHOLDER — sun rising, worker tapping a green button]*

- Once a day, open the app and tap **"I am safe today"**.
- If you miss check-ins, your NGO organization may call you to ask if
  everything is okay. That is care, not surveillance.
- **Emergency (HELP):** hold the emergency button in immediate danger. Your
  confirmed trusted contacts and the NGO team are alerted with your location
  (only if you allowed location).
- Trusted contacts: add up to 5 people in **My profile → Trusted contacts**.
  Each one must confirm, and you can send a **test message** first.
- Safety check-ins are **never rate-limited** — use them as often as needed.
- **Again: immediate danger = call 112 first.**

## 5. File a complaint

🖼️ *[ILLUSTRATION PLACEHOLDER — worker speaking, caseworker listening]*

1. **Complaints → File complaint.**
2. Pick what happened: unpaid/short wages, safety problem, or other.
3. Describe it in a few words — like telling a person, no format needed.
4. Optional: add evidence (photo/PDF, max 10 MB, up to 10 files per case).
   Files are checked for safety before caseworkers open them.
5. Submit. You immediately see a **case number**.

What happens next (the four stages — show the timeline):

```
1 Received → 2 Under review → 3 Action taken → 4 Resolved
```

- A human caseworker reviews every complaint — **nothing is auto-rejected**.
- You get a notification at every status change (bell icon 🔔).
- NGO notes and your evidence stay private. **Employers never see complaints,
  evidence, or check-ins** — they only ever see wage records you consented to
  share.

## 6. Questions and what happens next

Close the session:

- Open the worker's own app and let them do one action themselves
  (add a wage from today's work, if real).
- Hand out the pocket card (below) or write the helpline on paper.
- Sign-in sheet: name, phone, language chosen, signature.

## हिंदी सारांश (विज़ुअल सत्र — कम टेक्स्ट)

🖼️ *[प्रत्येक चरण के लिए चित्र/स्क्रीनशॉट स्थान]*

### 1. पंजीकरण — सिर्फ़ फ़ोन नंबर
- अपना मोबाइल नंबर डालें → **कोड पाएँ** → SMS का 6-अंकों वाला कोड भरें
- ईमेल या पासवर्ड की ज़रूरत नहीं
- भाषा चुनें: हिंदी / English / বাংলা / தமிழ் / తెలుగు

### 2. मजदूरी लिखें
- **मजदूरी → नई एंट्री**: तारीख़, रकम, काम की श्रेणी, नियोक्ता/साइट
- हर एंट्री आपका **अपना रिकॉर्ड** है
- कम मजदूरी दिखे तो ऐप पूछेगा — **शिकायत आप तय करते हैं, अपने-आप नहीं बनती**
- पहली बार डेटा देने से पहले एक छोटी **सहमति स्क्रीन** आएगी — पढ़कर सहमति दें

### 3. हर दिन सुरक्षा जांच
- हर दिन **"मैं सुरक्षित हूँ"** दबाएँ
- जांच छूट गई तो NGO संपर्क कर सकता है
- खतरे में: इमरजेंसी बटन + भरोसेमंद संपर्क (5 तक, पहले टेस्ट मैसेज)
- **तुरंत खतरा = 112**

### 4. शिकायत दर्ज करें
- **शिकायतें → नई शिकायत**: क्या हुआ + कुछ शब्दों में बताएँ
- फ़ोटो/PDF जोड़ सकते हैं (10 MB तक, प्रति केस 10 फ़ाइलें)
- केस नंबर मिलेगा, हर बदलाव पर 🔔 सूचना
- **हर शिकायत को इंसान देखता है — कोई शिकायत अपने-आप रद्द नहीं होती**
- नियोक्ता को कभी शिकायत/सबूत/चेक-इन नहीं दिखते

### 5. याद रखें
- Pehchaan आपात सेवा, पुलिस, अदालत या श्रम विभाग का **विकल्प नहीं** है
- आपका डेटा आपका है — प्रोफ़ाइल से **PDF/CSV/JSON** में डाउनलोड करें,
  चाहें तो डिलीट की मांग करें
