## Pehchaan — reliable information, faster action, fairer workplaces

**Pehchaan is a mobile platform that connects informal-sector workers with the
NGOs that support them — and gives verified employers a simple, private way to
demonstrate fair practice.**

For workers, Pehchaan is a simple phone app: a personal wage record, a daily
safety check-in, and a direct line to NGO caseworkers when something goes
wrong. For NGOs, it is an organized case-management inbox. For verified
employers, it is **credible, consent-based proof that their worksites treat
workers fairly — with none of their business data exposed.**

## Why a responsible employer would join

1. **Recognition you can point to.** Your worksite gets a verified QR code.
   Workers who scan it link your verified worksite to their own records —
   so payments you make are matched to your site, not lost in generic data.
2. **A fair hearing, not a scandal.** When a worker raises a problem, it goes
   to a trained NGO caseworker — not to social media. You get a structured,
   documented process instead of an unmanaged dispute.
3. **Aggregate standing only.** Employer compliance is ever shown only as an
   aggregate response signal. No public scoring, no penalties, no public
   shaming — investigations stay investigative.
4. **Zero data exposure.** Employers **never** see complaints, evidence,
   safety check-ins, case notes, or worker identities beyond the wage records
   the worker consented to share. Full stop.

## How it works

```
Worker logs wages ──┐
Worker checks in ───┼──► Pehchaan ──► NGO caseworkers review
Complaint filed ────┘        │
                             ▼
        Verified worksite QR links records to your site
                             ▼
        Aggregate compliance signal for verified employers
```

- A worker scans your worksite QR → their wage records can reference your
  verified worksite.
- Complaints referencing your site are handled by NGO caseworkers, who may
  contact you through a documented process.
- Disputes about wages are backed by the worker's own dated wage record —
  which usually resolves genuine confusion fast and in everyone's favour.

## What Pehchaan is not

- **Not an emergency service.** Pehchaan does not replace emergency services,
  police, courts, or labour departments.
- **Not a rating agency.** No public employer scores.
- **Not a reporting line for your competitors.** Data is shared on a
  need-to-know basis only, enforced by role permissions in the software.

## What we ask of verified employers

- Register honestly (company registration verified at approval).
- Respond constructively when a caseworker contacts you about a case.
- Keep the worksite QR at the site and inform workers what it is.
