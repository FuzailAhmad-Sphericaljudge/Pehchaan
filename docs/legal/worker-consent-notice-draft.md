# Worker Consent Notice — DRAFT (on-screen text)

> **STATUS: DRAFT — PENDING LEGAL REVIEW**
> This is the exact text shown on screen before a worker's first data
> submission (Phase 15 consent flow, Phase 35 text). It is deliberately
> written at a reading level for a first-time smartphone user — short
> sentences, no legal jargon. Must be reviewed and approved by a qualified
> lawyer. The editable copy lives in the CMS (`worker-consent`, locales
> `en` and `hi`); this file is the review copy.

## Where it appears

- The worker app shows this notice once, in a gate screen ("Before you
  start"), before the worker can save a wage entry, check-in, or complaint.
- The acknowledgement is recorded on the device (timestamp only — Pehchaan
  does not add a consent record to the worker's data).
- The full policy is always reachable from the gate screen and the footer
  (`/worker-consent`, `/privacy-policy`, `/terms-of-use`).

## English (on screen)

**Before you start**

- What you save (wages, check-ins, complaints, photos) is stored so you have
  your own proof.
- A caseworker of the NGO helping you can see your complaint and photos.
  Others cannot.
- An employer sees only the wage records you agree to share. Never your
  complaints.
- Your location is shared only when you allow it for a check-in or complaint.
- You can download or delete your record anytime from the app.

Nothing here is a guarantee. In an emergency, call your local emergency
number first.

Button: **I understand — continue**
Link: **Read the full details: privacy notice and consent**

## Review notes for the lawyer

1. Does this notice satisfy the consent standard for digital consent under
   applicable Indian law (information technology rules), given the target
   users' literacy and language needs?
2. Should the acknowledgement be recorded server-side (with a version
   reference to the notice text) rather than only on-device?
3. Is the "on-device only" consent sufficient if the worker changes phones?
4. Confirm the emergency-services disclaimer wording matches the platform's
   risk position.
